📘 Introduction

What to Expect

Welcome to the course. Whether you are brand new to trading or tired of overcomplicated YouTube videos, you are in the right place.

This course is built differently. No fake flexing, no confusing Wall Street language, and no get-rich-quick promises. The goal is to help you understand trading in a simple, visual, and practical way.

You will learn how to read charts, understand price movement, use basic tools, manage risk, and build better trading habits. By the end, you should have a clearer idea of how to approach the market with patience, structure, and discipline.

Take it one lesson at a time. Trading is a skill, and skills are built through repetition. 👊

🧰 What You’ll Need

  • ✅ A notepad, journal, Google Sheet, or Notion page
  • ✅ A free TradingView account
  • ✅ Internet access and about 15–30 minutes per lesson
  • ✅ Patience, because trading is learned through practice

📊 Lesson 2: Reading Charts

In this module, you will learn the chart basics every beginner should understand. We will cover candlesticks, support and resistance, market structure, and confirmation tools so you can stop looking at charts like random lines.

Candlesticks show you how price moved during a specific period of time. You will learn bullish candles, bearish candles, doji candles, engulfing candles, hammers, and other beginner-friendly signals.

Support and resistance are areas where price has reacted before. These zones can help you understand where buyers and sellers may step in.

Volume helps you see whether a move has strength behind it. When used with structure and key levels, it can help you avoid weak setups and false breakouts.
